# Workflow Semantics

Machine-readable stage order lives in `config/workflow.json`.

## Stage intent
- INTAKE: define task, constraints, platform, target viewer and evidence requirements.
- POSITIONING: confirm the topic strengthens the account's long-term commercial identity.
- RESEARCH: collect enough evidence to understand the mechanism, not merely restate headlines.
- RESEARCH_AUDIT: independently attack evidence quality and overclaiming.
- SCRIPT: create hook, narrative spine and payoff.
- SCRIPT_AUDIT: test comprehension, retention and factual wording.
- STORYBOARD: convert claims into shots with explicit visual jobs.
- VISUAL_PREVIEW: render a short/high-risk sample before expensive full production.
- ASSET_BUILD: build/gather approved assets and record rights/provenance.
- ROUGH_CUT: assemble timing and remove dead weight.
- SOUND: design voice/music/SFX/silence as a coordinated layer.
- TECH_QA: probe the real final media, not project metadata.
- AUDIENCE_QA: cold-viewer review after the actual cut exists.
- BRAND_QA: judge whether the work strengthens advertiser-facing identity.
- RED_TEAM: produce the strongest case against publishing.
- RELEASE: showrunner signs only after every upstream hard gate is valid.
- PERFORMANCE: capture real platform evidence after publication.
- LEARN: decide what becomes reusable knowledge, what remains local and what should be rejected.
- COMPLETE: durable project package exists.

## Downstream invalidation
Material changes to a stage invalidate all approvals at that stage and downstream. Examples: changing the thesis invalidates script onward; changing the music mix invalidates SOUND onward; replacing a key visual may invalidate ROUGH_CUT onward.
