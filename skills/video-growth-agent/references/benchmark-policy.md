# Benchmark and Lookdev Policy

## Purpose
Calibrate taste before production. The benchmark pack is a measuring instrument, not an inspiration dump.

## Required pack
At least 3 references: same-format, world-class craft, and explanation/clarity. At least one reference must be evaluated as **moving audiovisual work**, not only as isolated stills. Each entry records URL/source, why it matters, 3–7 observable traits, which dimensions it calibrates, and a `do_not_copy` note.

Do not choose references merely because they use the same AI model. Prefer work whose composition, shot design, asset continuity, motion, sound, edit and final polish are visibly stronger than the current candidate.

## Required style bible
Define palette, type scale, material/texture, light, depth/lens, grid, icon/illustration language, motion grammar, transition grammar, subtitle language, sound intent, and forbidden clichés. “Dark background + neon outlines + floating cards” is forbidden as a default premium-tech shortcut unless the benchmark pack specifically justifies it.

## Required hero frames
At minimum: hook, mechanism/reveal, payoff. Render them near final quality before full animation. The three frames should not look as if they came from three templates.

## Prototype reel before full build
After hero frames but before a full rough cut, render a **3–12 second near-final sample reel** that tests the most expensive or failure-prone part of the intended film. A normal target is 5–12 seconds.

The sample is not a technical animatic. It must represent the intended final language:

- near-final visual assets/materials;
- representative camera, motion and transition grammar;
- representative typography/captions;
- representative sound/voice/music or deliberate silence;
- at least one high-risk reveal, interaction, continuity problem or complex shot;
- final-target aspect ratio and approximately final playback cadence.

If the sample would be embarrassing to show publicly as a fragment of the final work, stop. Do not build 60 seconds hoping polish later will rescue a weak 8 seconds.

## Exploration vs production
Use two lanes when useful:

- **exploration lane** — infinite canvas / moodboard / variant generation / model experiments; cheap, broad and disposable;
- **production lane** — approved references, locked assets, shot IDs, keyframes, audio intent, provenance and versioned revisions.

Ideas may move from exploration into production only after explicit selection. Production artifacts must not silently drift because a new exploratory generation looks interesting.

## Review behavior
Blind first pass: reviewers do not read prior verdicts. They inspect the current artifact and benchmark pack. Score 1–5 using `quality-rubric.json`, cite the target digest, and state blockers. No deferred-quality PASS.

For motion samples, reviewers must watch at normal speed and inspect contact frames. Comparing only screenshots is insufficient because weak timing, floaty motion and poor picture-sound relationships can hide in stills.
