## Author adversarial script critique (NOT isolated critic)

**1. Substitution test**: A pair of human soldiers could also voluntarily withhold fire, but this film requires three *uncommanded machine plan changes across multiple tactical units*: each AI must discover the feedback pattern itself, each must withhold despite newly available shots, and human commanders do not negotiate. If the actual sequence can be replaced by two human gunners without changing causal steps, **REWRITE or REJECT**. Distinct from a false “AI has special soul” claim.

**2. Cheap enemy alternative**: If either Agent could fire one guaranteed one-sided shot and end opposition with no expected retaliation to its own medical/provisioning vehicles, it should; the drama then collapses. Shootout must visibly demonstrate crossfire and loss in 00:20–00:31; cameras at 01:16 must show each side's vulnerable human objective. If still not convincing, reset premise rather than adding arbitrary immunity or a line of exposition.

**3. Story-specific human POV**: Sister has her own choice: she throws the glove as signal, which nearly attracts targeting, then chooses to test the new silence with a tin can, risks the crossing and deliberately leaves the paired glove behind to protect brother. His agency: he restrains her when she wants to return for the glove. Both are actual decisions, not only targets carried by machines.

**4. Weakest point**: Why does blue Agent with an offensive capture goal choose not to exploit the pause? Its equally explicit goal to limit human loss supplies a reason, but a sustained pause can let red escape and defeat its local goal. It must be shown that red also preserves threat capability; blue cannot safely claim free victory. Counterfactual "snipe opposing drone now" remains a live editorial veto until proven in action.

**5. Expected visual grammar**: Six fast mirrored gunshot pairs form an audiovisual rule; the *seventh absence* becomes a reveal that invites the girl to act. If a stranger perceives only random gunfire and then arbitrary silence, the whole film is just an authored lucky break and **FAILS**.

**6. Original Agent versus automated trigger**: The silence cannot be presented as universally programmed “do not fire on civilians.” The model of the opponent and changing own-risk calculus must precede the appearance of the crossing girl. Retrospective explanation is forbidden; behavior must tell it.

**7. Timing**: Physical removal of bicycle and walking a short damaged-person crossing in 00:76–01:43 (27 seconds) is a theatrical estimate, not a measured performance. 110 seconds is a script target. Never use provider’s 5/10-second clip length to redefine beats.

## Non-fiction proof versus fiction
The autonomous plans and hardware are invented. This is **fictional dramatic causality**, not an operational description of real military AI or a conclusion that autonomous weapons can broker reliable truces. No promotion of wartime tactics is intended or supported.
