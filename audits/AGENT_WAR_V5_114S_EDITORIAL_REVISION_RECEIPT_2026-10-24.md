Their actual strategic belief (a credible decisive strike may clear crossing) must be defeated by an observed failed exchange. Otherwise delayed caution looks arbitrary.
3. **Agent specificity**: Could two trained human escort officers devise this no-radio alternating-convoy plan in time? Show independently timed micro-predictions + contingent autonomous actions, not merely two vehicles politely yielding.
4. **Viewer focus**: Need audience to follow an emotional rescue AND two conflicting agency loops in 114 sec. If the audience only remembers the girl, reduce military exposition to one more legible cause/action pair and sharpen visual identity.
5. **Physical performance**: Four separately moving UAVs, one-lane geography, reverse bay, bicycle/lodged trouser removal, two vehicle transits — must fit a real time-accurate storyboard, not just prose duration labels.
6. **Ethics/warring sides**: Agent trading temporary non-fire because soldiers are in vehicles is **not** evidence that it values enemy civilians. Do not falsely claim moral awakening or mission override.
