## Completed / provisionally locked

### Account positioning
- Core editorial field: technology × business/capital × internet culture.
- Professional/commercial anchor: AI, chips, smart hardware, smart vehicles and technology-company/business stories.
- Meme/internet culture is a differentiation layer rather than a crypto-alpha identity.
- Primary early format hypothesis: Bilibili-oriented 6–10 minute AI-heavy cinematic explainers / mini-documentaries.
- Short-platform outputs are re-authored derivatives rather than the sole primary format.
- Platform payout is supplemental; commercial target is native sponsorship, brand integration, rights/licensing, series sponsorship and later commerce where natural.

### Production economics
- Goal: high AI visual share + low creator labor.
- AI visual share is not equal to premium video-generation share.
- Use model routing, retry budgets and graceful degradation rather than one-vendor lock-in or unlimited regeneration.
- Expensive video models are reserved for shots where motion quality materially matters.
- Real product truth uses authorized real/verified brand imagery where factual appearance/function matters.

### Character design direction
Rejected:
- blue-haired photoreal creator / cosplay;
- generic handsome AAA/K-idol male;
- literal dog ears, dog face, fish tail or furry treatment;
- shallow feature-copying / adjective-only meme treatment.

Accepted core concept:
> A high-resolution observer carrying low-resolution anomalies.

Reference-role split:
- pixel avatar = first-read identity DNA;
- Shiba-carp meme = hybrid/absurdity grammar, deadpan performance, material/shape contradiction and tiered easter-egg system;
- real creator = weak physical host anchor only: Chinese adult male, mid-20s, 182cm/67kg tall-slim, warm-neutral somewhat deeper East Asian skin tone, long limbs, non-muscular.

Accepted large-form design principles:
- vertical I-shaped silhouette, natural/narrow shoulders, long legs;
- non-generic memorable face, long narrow eyes, restrained asymmetry and deadpan mouth/brow read;
- hair designed as five large masses before individual strands, asymmetric front locks, subtle non-animal triangular rhythm and split rear flow;
- black/white pixel sunglasses remain deliberately low-resolution and geometrically incompatible with the otherwise high-resolution character;
- costume architecture is mostly geometric/engineered, with one structural path transitioning into organic flow;
- offset two-part rear hem, hidden Pixel→Scale interior textile and tiny warm anomaly-color system;
- easter eggs are tiered and not all visible on first read.

### Prompt / image-generation methodology
- Do not use adjective piles as authority.
- Translate narrative intent into observable visual parameters.
- Separate narrative function, first/second/third read, silhouette, shape language, face, hair, grooming, costume, motif fusion, easter eggs, color/material hierarchy, pose/performance, camera/light/render, exclusions and output contract.
- Do not ask one generation pass to solve every final asset.
- Validate large forms first, then keep the same identity and converge through controlled edits.
