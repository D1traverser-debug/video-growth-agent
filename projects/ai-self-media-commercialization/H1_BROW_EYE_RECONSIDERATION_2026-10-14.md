# H1 Brow–Eye Reconsideration — 2026-10-14

Status: ACTIVE / BROW-EYE DESIGN PASS INSERTED BEFORE 3/4
Scope: H1 Human Core face geometry

## Trigger

After reviewing the corrected H1-S13 front sculpt, the user noted that the result still feels very close to the creator and specifically questioned whether the creator-derived brow/eye system should be preserved, because it does not match the desired level of attractiveness / character appeal.

## Independent design judgment

Do not preserve the creator's brow and eye appearance wholesale.

The project should preserve only source ancestry that is structurally useful:
- interocular spacing / orbital placement tendency;
- broadly horizontal brow-eye organization;
- adult Chinese male plausibility.

The following are DESIGN VARIABLES, not mandatory likeness anchors:
- brow thickness and silhouette;
- brow arch / tail trajectory;
- brow-eye distance within a natural range;
- eye aperture height;
- horizontal eye length;
- upper-lid curvature;
- lower-lid tension;
- subtle canthal tilt;
- visible sclera / iris balance.

Reason:
The IP target is a same-source original, not a digital double. The current sculpt is valuable as a face-shell / massing candidate, but its brow-eye system remains too close to the source and should be deliberately redesigned for character appeal, calm intelligence and long-term recognizability.

## Design principle

Do NOT solve this by generic beauty optimization (larger eyes, heroic brows, fox-eye tilt, idol makeup, deep-set fashion-model orbit).

Instead, keep the current face shell fixed and compare three controlled brow-eye systems:

### E1 — Source Quiet Control
- the CURRENT corrected S1+S3 front sculpt itself is E1;
- do NOT regenerate E1;
- keep it as the source-like control image for comparison;
- restrained vertical aperture, natural near-horizontal brow, current source-like brow-eye read.

### E2 — Refined Analytical (recommended)
- preserve eye spacing and orbital placement;
- increase perceived horizontal eye length slightly without enlarging vertical opening;
- make the upper lid line cleaner and calmer;
- keep outer canthus nearly level, at most a very slight rise;
- lower lid slightly firmer / more deliberate but not sharp;
- brow medium density, straighter and cleaner than source, with a subtle lift only in the outer third;
- brow tail slightly more resolved/extended;
- keep brow-eye distance naturally compact without aggression.

Target read: intelligent, calm, adult, attractive without idolization, observant rather than seductive or hostile.

### E3 — Editorial Quiet
- preserve spacing and basic orbit placement;
- eye becomes a little longer and flatter than E2;
- vertical opening remains restrained;
- brow is flatter, more graphic and slightly more architectural;
- stronger design read than E2 but still fully human.

Risk: can drift toward runway/fashion-face abstraction and reduce warmth/commercial universality.

## Recommendation

E2 is the leading recommendation, but do not lock it without a controlled visual comparison against E1 and E3.
