## Core finding

The text-only F1–F5 experiment was useful, but all five outputs partially collapsed toward the same GPT prior: an attractive, conventionally masculine East-Asian studio portrait. The intended architectural differences were visible, but weaker than planned. Therefore the project should NOT pick a winner merely from attractiveness or continue to 3/4 unchanged.

The experiment demonstrates that:
1. structure-only text prompting can explore broad directions without cloning the creator;
2. however, text-only morphology is insufficient to preserve enough same-source facial relationships once GPT beautification / generic-male priors activate;
3. the next H1 step should be a controlled hybrid + certificate-photo calibration, not a blind winner-take-all selection.

## Independent recommendation

Do not advance F1–F5 as standalone finalists.

Create one deliberate hybrid architecture `H1-HYBRID-A`:
- base face envelope / soft-tissue naturalness from F1;
- eye aperture and low-expression read from F4;
- cheek→jaw→compact-chin transition logic from F5;
- certificate portrait used only to calibrate source-specific face length/width tendency, brow-eye relationship, eye spacing, nose-mouth proportion and cheek/jaw/chin distribution.

Explicit anti-generic corrections for Hybrid A:
- soften the brow ridge and avoid thick heroic eyebrows;
- keep eyes horizontal and moderately narrow without fashion-model sharpening;
- keep nose medium and natural, not narrow/high/straightened;
- keep lips quiet and unsculpted;
- maintain cheek support but avoid macho jaw broadening;
- keep chin compact, blunt/rounded and not pointed;
- do not optimize conventional handsomeness.
