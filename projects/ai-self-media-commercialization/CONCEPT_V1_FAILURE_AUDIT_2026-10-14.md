# Concept V1 Failure Audit — 2026-10-14

Status: REJECTED
Scope: project-local character design
Stage consequence: CHARACTER_CONCEPT_DESIGN_V1 -> CHARACTER_REDESIGN_RESEARCH_V2

## Executive conclusion

The first full-body concept is rejected as a whole, not merely as a styling miss.

The failure is systemic across face, body archetype, hair, costume, accessory scale, pose and overall art direction. It should not be repaired by local retouching or by generating a closer face crop from the same design logic.

The image is technically clean but aesthetically generic. Its strongest read is "blue-haired techwear male model / game promo character", which is explicitly outside the target identity.

## Failure class

Primary failure classes:
- MACRO_CHARACTER_DESIGN_FAILURE
- BODY_ARCHETYPE_FAILURE
- COSTUME_SILHOUETTE_FAILURE
- GENERIC_FACE_DESIGN_FAILURE
- HAIR_SHAPE_FAILURE
- SIGNATURE_ACCESSORY_UNDERSCALE
- POSE_AND_DIRECTING_FAILURE
- MOTIF_FUSION_FAILURE

This is not a prompt wording bug only. The underlying design authority was under-specified at macro-form level and over-specified at micro-detail/easter-egg level.

## Face audit

Rejected traits:
- narrow, polished, generic CG handsome-male face;
- pointed/elongated lower-face tendency;
- insufficiently distinctive facial massing;
- attractive but replaceable eye/nose/mouth arrangement;
- styling reads closer to idol/game-promo aesthetics than analytical adult observer;
- deadpan concept is not embodied in facial construction; it is only an expression overlay.

Root cause:
The prior bible tried to create identity with eyelid asymmetry, brow offset and mouth-corner micro-adjustments before establishing a stronger primary face shape system.

Correction principle:
Lock primary facial masses first: skull width/height, brow plane, cheek mass, jaw/chin mass, eye-socket placement, nose volume and mouth-to-jaw relationship. Micro-asymmetry is tertiary and cannot carry identity.

## Body audit

Rejected traits:
- tall/slim was interpreted as generic fashion-model elongation rather than a designed character body;
- shoulders, ribcage, pelvis and limb relationships do not create a memorable body archetype;
- jacket volume obscures the intended real-world body anchor;
- stance reads as fashion posing rather than an observer occupying a scene;
- hands-in-pockets pose collapses performance identity into lookbook language.

Root cause:
"182cm / 67kg / tall-slim" was treated as a numeric instruction rather than a shape-language problem.

Correction principle:
The V2 body must explicitly design head-to-body ratio, shoulder-to-pelvis ratio, ribcage/pelvis widths, neck length, arm/leg proportion, hand/foot scale, center of gravity and neutral gesture. Body silhouette must work without clothing first.

## Hair audit

Rejected traits:
- diffuse spiky salon/game hair;
- too many small strands and silhouette noise;
- insufficient separation between primary masses;
- no unmistakable front/back mass hierarchy;
- the intended motif language is not readable without explanation.

Root cause:
The design described five masses verbally but did not enforce a mass-first visual test.

Correction principle:
V2 hair must be judged first as a flat black silhouette and only then as strands/material. If the silhouette is not distinctive at thumbnail size, hair design fails.

## Costume audit

Rejected traits:
- generic white/black/cyan techwear;
- too many small zippers, tabs, pockets, panels and blue accents;
- jacket construction lacks a clear dominant proportion idea;
- asymmetry appears decorative rather than structural;
- trousers are ordinary tactical/cargo-adjacent pants;
- shoes are generic technical sneakers;
- rear tail element reads as an added flap rather than an integrated garment architecture.

Root cause:
The design jumped to surface detailing before solving garment silhouette, proportion, balance and fabric behavior.

Correction principle:
V2 costume order:
1. body silhouette;
2. garment silhouette over body;
3. proportion and balance;
4. dominant lines;
5. fabric weight/drape;
6. functional openings/closures;
7. only then motif details and easter eggs.

The next garment concept must survive a no-detail blockout test.

## Pixel sunglasses audit

Rejected traits:
- too small to carry first-read identity;
- functions as a novelty accessory instead of a rule-breaking visual object;
- visual weight is weaker than hair and jacket details.

Correction principle:
Scale and shape must be designed at head-silhouette level. The accessory must remain readable in a thumbnail and must materially interrupt the otherwise continuous character language.

## Motif / Shiba-carp audit

Rejected result:
The meme reference is not perceptible as an integrated design grammar. There is no meaningful discovered layer after the first read.

Root cause:
Previous direction still relied too much on explanatory notes and easter-egg inventory instead of letting the meme alter major forms, motion rhythm and staging.

Correction principle:
Do not begin by adding more Shiba/fish details. First define one macro contradiction that changes silhouette or motion, one medium-scale contradiction that changes garment/hair structure, and one micro easter-egg layer. If the design requires verbal explanation to prove the reference is present, the fusion is too weak.

## Pose / direction audit

Rejected traits:
- fashion lookbook stance;
- hands in pockets;
- passive body display;
- no clear narrative action or observation behavior.

Correction principle:
Character reference poses must separate identity truth from fashion styling. Neutral canon pose should expose body construction and garment balance. Performance poses should show observation, analysis and restrained reaction, not model attitude.

## What remains valid

Still valid as broad project DNA only:
- adult Chinese male host anchor;
- tall/slim real-world origin;
- ice-cyan / blue family;
- deep blue-violet eye concept;
- black/white pixel accessory concept;
- technology × business × internet-culture narrative role;
- deadpan observer principle;
- controlled easter-egg hierarchy;
- no literal animal hybridization.

Everything else is open for redesign.

## Anti-regression rule

Do not use this rejected concept as a visual reference for V2 generation.
Do not crop its face and refine it.
Do not preserve its jacket/pants/shoes.
Do not treat "clean render" or "commercial-looking" as evidence of design quality.

The next phase must redesign macro forms from first principles.
