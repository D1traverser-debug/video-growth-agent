# Avatar Studio V5 — Historical Snapshot (2026-11-11)

Source: private Google Doc https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-69ZRY-eyNaow0ds7qiRir4sNIAe9PgTO_ekIdWPpVo/edit

This is a historical pre-R30 snapshot. It must not override the later 2026-11-12 R30/R31 state.

## Stable requirements
- Main avatar supports English X (Solana meme, global trends, entertainment), later Chinese WeChat (A-share and technology) and future video.
- Digital human needs brand association, not the same face.
- Keep complete 1:1 square source canvas; do not circle-crop the master.
- User photos may inform abstracted facial/age/temperament features; do not publish an identifiable face by default.
- Rejected styles: template anime handsome male, chin-rest pose, blue-gold highlights, glowing background, K-lines, meaningless gold-scale rings, sticker dog, cheap flat cartoon, and 4K upscaling used to hide weak design.

## Historical research areas
Disney Animation creative/visual development; Pixar visual language; V&A illustration interview with James Albon; Malika Favre and Noma Bar on shape/negative space; Ehrenberg-Bass distinctive brand assets; Nielsen Norman Group icon testing; OpenAI image prompting; X profile image requirements.
