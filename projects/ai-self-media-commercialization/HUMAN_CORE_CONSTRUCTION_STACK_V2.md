# Human Core Construction Stack — V2

Status: ACTIVE_METHOD / PRE-CANON
Scope: project-local only
Purpose: define the human-character construction authority before clothing, runway styling, presentation modes, or final generation.

## 0. Core correction

Presentation modes are downstream derivatives. They are **not** the current design object.

The dependency order is:

1. HUMAN CORE — face, skull, body, skin, hair baseline, neutral posture/movement;
2. IDENTITY DNA — pixel-avatar color/signature object and nonliteral motif rules;
3. WARDROBE CHASSIS — tailoring/drape/garment silhouette;
4. SIGNATURE DETAIL SYSTEM — accessory, motif, controlled easter eggs;
5. CANON CHARACTER — neutral identity truth;
6. PRESENTATION MODES — editorial, cinematic, surreal, commercial variants.

No presentation mode may compensate for an unresolved human core.

## 1. What counts as the Human Core

The Human Core is the character with no fashion styling advantage and no hero lighting advantage.

It must remain recognizable in:
- neutral close face;
- neutral fitted base clothing;
- grayscale;
- black hair / dark eyes substitution test;
- no pixel sunglasses test;
- front / 3-quarter / profile;
- neutral standing pose.

Human Core components:
- cranial and facial primary masses;
- eye socket / brow / cheek / jaw / chin architecture;
- nose and mouth volumes;
- neck, shoulder, ribcage, waist, pelvis and limb proportions;
- hand/foot scale;
- neutral skin value/undertone;
- baseline male grooming;
- hair architecture as a head-shape system, not fashion strand noise;
- neutral posture and movement signature.

## 2. Reference-role policy

### Real certificate photo — STRUCTURAL FACE SOURCE ONLY
Use for:
- face length/width tendency;
- brow-to-eye relationship;
- eye spacing and orbital placement tendency;
- nose-to-mouth proportion tendency;
- cheek/jaw/chin distribution;
- adult Chinese male age/gender anchor.

Explicitly ignore:
- makeup;
- retouching;
- whitening;
- beauty smoothing;
- hairstyle;
- suit/formal styling;
- photographic color cast.

Important execution rule:
Do not make the certificate photo the dominant direct image-generation reference for the first V2 face concept. First extract morphology into a written/diagrammatic face-structure sheet, then generate from that sheet. This reduces the risk of returning to “real creator with blue hair.”

### Real life / sports / full-body photos — BODY AND SKIN TRUTH SOURCE
Use for:
- true skin value and warm-neutral undertone;
- real shoulder slope;
- torso/leg relation;
- limb length;
- real 182cm/67kg tall-slim origin;
- posture and adult male physical presence.

Do not copy casual clothing or temporary pose as canon styling.

### Pixel avatar — BRAND/GRAPHIC DNA SOURCE
Use for:
- ice-cyan / cyan-blue head color family;
- deep blue-violet iris concept;
- black/white pixel sunglasses;
- white / ice-blue / black identity family;
- pixel / internet-native / geek lineage;
- optional original red as rare anomaly accent.

Do not use for:
- facial anatomy;
- body anatomy;
- literal pixel-art proportions;
- gender ambiguity in the source sprite.

### Shiba-carp meme — NARRATIVE / CONTRADICTION SOURCE
Use for:
- impossible hybrid treated as normal;
- deadpan reaction grammar;
- mismatch between two rule systems;
- later macro/meso/micro motif logic.

Do not use as an anatomy source.

## 3. Personal-feature fusion policy

Recommendation: **yes, fuse the creator, but through selected structural inheritance rather than face-copying.**

Three inheritance tiers:

### Tier A — must inherit
- Chinese adult male identity;
- mid-20s age band;
- tall/slim physical origin;
- natural warm-neutral somewhat deeper skin value;
- real shoulder/limb plausibility;
- analytical, non-performative physical presence.

### Tier B — selectively inherit after comparison
- face aspect ratio tendency;
- eye spacing / brow-eye relation;
- nose-mouth spacing;
- cheek/jaw relation;
- one or two natural asymmetries if they support identity.

Tier B is used to create “same-source resonance,” not likeness replication.

### Tier C — do not inherit
- certificate-photo makeup/retouch;
- beauty filter / whitening;
- current real hairstyle;
- current wardrobe;
- accidental expression;
- camera/lens distortion;
- any feature that pushes the character back toward a direct digital double.

## 4. Specialist role stack

These are capability roles, not proof that separate agents executed. Any operational use must have an actual invocation/evidence path.

### Human Core Director
Owns the integrated human identity and rejects downstream styling that masks weak anatomy.

### Facial Morphology / Character Sculptor
Owns skull, facial primary masses, planes, profile and landmark relations before beautification.

### Body Anatomy & Proportion Designer
Owns head-to-body ratio, neck/shoulder/ribcage/pelvis/limb relations, center of gravity, hands/feet and neutral gesture.

### Hair Architecture Specialist
Owns cut geometry, line/graduation/layering, weight distribution and front/profile silhouette.

### Grooming / Makeup Director
Owns skin preparation logic, value/undertone, shine control, brows, orbital depth, lips and camera readability without idol homogenization.

### Fashion / Tailoring / Runway Designer
Downstream of Human Core. Owns silhouette, proportion, balance, construction, drape, deconstruction and staging; may not redefine anatomy to rescue garments.

### Character Acting / Movement Director
Owns posture, gesture vocabulary, gait, head behavior and deadpan performance signature.

### IP Motif / Semiotics Designer
Owns pixel-avatar and Shiba-carp translation into brand grammar without literal feature pasting.

### Generative Consistency Engineer
Owns reference-role separation, prompt compilation, identity-stability tests, model drift checks and edit-vs-regenerate decisions.

### Commercial Art Director / Brand Fit Reviewer
Checks long-term sponsorship adaptability without flattening distinctiveness.

### Genericness / Regression Red Team
Actively searches for K-idol, game-promo, techwear, cosplay, runway-mannequin, mascot, and generic-AI shortcuts.

## 6. Harness / gate sequence

### H0 — REFERENCE DECOMPOSITION
Output:
- face morphology sheet from real portrait;
- body/skin truth sheet from life photos;
- pixel-avatar DNA sheet;
- Shiba-carp narrative/motif sheet.

Pass condition:
Each reference has explicit allowed and forbidden influence.

### H1 — FACE PRIMARY MASSES
Compare multiple face architectures in neutral grayscale, without cyan hair, colored eyes, sunglasses or fashion.

Pass condition:
Face is memorable without branding props and does not read as generic idol/game male or a direct copy of the creator.

### H2 — BASE BODY ARCHETYPE
Compare body silhouettes in fitted neutral base clothing.

Pass condition:
Adult male, tall/light but stable; distinctive posture/proportion; not fashion-mannequin elongation or weak adolescent body.

### H3 — HAIR ARCHITECTURE
Front + profile black-silhouette test before color/strand detail.

Pass condition:
Recognizable head silhouette at thumbnail scale and compatible with the approved skull/face.

### H4 — SKIN / GROOMING
Add true skin value, brow/eye/lip treatment and camera-ready grooming.

Pass condition:
Face planes remain readable; grooming does not turn the character into a beauty-ad/K-idol template.

### H5 — IDENTITY STRIP TEST
Test the approved human core with:
- dark hair;
- dark eyes;
- no sunglasses;
- grayscale.

Pass condition:
It still reads as the same character.

### H6 — BRAND DNA REINTRODUCTION
Add cyan hair family, deep blue-violet irises and pixel sunglasses.

Pass condition:
Brand DNA strengthens identity rather than creating it from scratch.

### H7 — WARDROBE CHASSIS
Only now compare tailoring/drape/runway-derived garment systems.

Pass condition:
Garment silhouette supports the approved body and communicates character without techwear-detail clutter.

### H8 — MOTIF FUSION
Add macro/meso contradiction first, micro easter eggs later.

Pass condition:
Shiba-carp lineage is felt through rule mismatch/structure/motion without literal animal cosplay.

### H9 — CANON CHARACTER
Build canonical face, full body, turnarounds, neutral expression and motion baseline.

### H10 — PRESENTATION MODES
Only after H9:
- CANON;
- EDITORIAL;
- CINEMATIC;
- SURREAL;
- COMMERCIAL.

Modes may alter styling, makeup intensity, clothing, staging and lensing. They may not alter the Human Core identity.

## 8. Presentation-mode policy

Presentation modes remain **defined but dormant** until H9 CANON CHARACTER passes.

Do not design separate editorial/surreal/commercial faces now.
Do not use runway makeup or extreme styling to decide what the base person looks like.
Runway/fashion research is allowed now only as a source of proportion, attitude, silhouette theory and later wardrobe possibilities.
