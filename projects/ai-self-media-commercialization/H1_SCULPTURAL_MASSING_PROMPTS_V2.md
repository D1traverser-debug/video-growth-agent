# H1 Sculptural Massing Prompts — V2

Status: PROPOSED / READY FOR EXECUTION AFTER REVIEW
Scope: H1 Face Primary Masses only
Use with: creator certificate portrait uploaded in a fresh GPT Images conversation.

## Method

Switch representation domain from photoreal portrait to neutral sculptural maquette so skin, hair, grooming and conventional attractiveness cannot hide the facial geometry.

Generate THREE separate studies, one per fresh conversation. Use the same certificate portrait in each, but state that it is a structural reference only.

Common presentation lock:
- monochrome matte-gray clay head / maquette;
- strict front view;
- head + upper neck;
- eye-level, long-lens perspective;
- no realistic skin texture;
- no makeup;
- no hair strands; use a close-fitting simple blocked hair mass or neutral scalp cap;
- no eyebrow hair detail; brows read only as low-relief mass/plane;
- no eyelashes;
- no iris color / glossy eyes; eyes rendered as neutral sculpt geometry;
- no clothing/fashion identity;
- simple mid-gray background;
- large soft neutral light;
- no cinematic grading;
- no text/collage.

The uploaded certificate portrait may calibrate only:
- face width/length tendency;
- brow-eye placement;
- interocular spacing;
- cheek width and mass distribution;
- nose/mouth placement and relative volume;
- jaw/chin distribution.

Do not reproduce the photographed person. Do not preserve hairstyle, makeup, skin, expression, suit or retouching.

## S1 — Source-Calibrated Neutral

Create a neutral clay head study for an original mid-20s Chinese male Human Core.

Keep the source-derived structure conservative:
- moderately elongated soft-rectangular/oval envelope;
- natural cheek support;
- gradual lower-face taper;
- compact rounded-blunt chin;
- horizontal brow/eye organization;
- moderate-narrow orbital opening;
- medium natural nose volume;
- quiet moderate mouth volume;
- present but soft jaw.

Goal: establish the most plausible same-source structural baseline without beauty optimization.

Reject V-face, heroic brow, model nose, macho jaw, fashion face or direct likeness cloning.

## S2 — Structural Compression

Use the same source-derived baseline, but make only these geometric changes:
- slightly reduce perceived lower-face vertical length;
- make the chin slightly shorter and more compact while remaining rounded-blunt;
- reduce orbital vertical opening slightly while keeping the eyes horizontal and healthy;
- preserve cheek width and jaw plausibility;
- do not broaden the jaw.

Goal: stronger natural deadpan readability through geometry alone, without sleepy or cute proportions.

## S3 — Anti-Generic Cheek/Jaw Rhythm

Use the same source-derived baseline, but make only these geometric changes:
- preserve or slightly emphasize lateral cheek support;
- keep the upper jaw/lower cheek from collapsing inward too quickly;
- create a subtly less conventional transition from cheek to jaw to compact rounded-blunt chin;
- keep the jaw moderate, not square or wide;
- keep brow, nose, mouth and eye placement conservative.

Goal: introduce a faint second-look distinctiveness through the cheek→jaw→chin rhythm only, while remaining fully believable human anatomy.

## Review criteria

Compare S1-S3 only on:
- skull/face envelope;
- cheek mass;
- jaw taper;
- chin volume;
- orbital opening and placement;
- nose/mouth relative volume;
- adult male credibility;
- anti-generic distinctiveness;
- suitability for later stylized skin/hair reconstruction.

Do not evaluate attractiveness, skin, hairstyle or fashion.

Advance rule:
- if one geometry clearly wins, use it as H1 base;
- if two each have useful geometry, explicitly hybridize those masses;
- do not return to photoreal skin/hair until a sculpt geometry is selected;
- do not advance to 3/4 until the front sculpt geometry is accepted.
