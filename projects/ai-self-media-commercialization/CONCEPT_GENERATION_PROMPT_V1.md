# Concept Generation Prompt — V1

Use this only after providing the following references in a fresh image-generation conversation:
- R1: original pixel avatar;
- R2: Shiba-carp meme image;
- R3: creator ID/photo face reference;
- R4: real full-body/lifestyle reference.

The reference roles below are strict. Do not let the real photographs dictate final rendering style.

```text
Design one original male commercial digital-IP character from the supplied references.

REFERENCE PRIORITY

R1 pixel avatar defines the first-read visual DNA only:
- ice-cyan / cyan-blue hair;
- deep blue-violet irises;
- black-and-white pixel sunglasses;
- white / ice-blue / black system;
- digital-native, geek and light meme lineage.

R2 Shiba-carp meme does NOT provide literal animal anatomy.
Do not create dog ears, dog face, fish tail, furry features or fish scales on skin.
It provides a deeper design grammar:
"an extremely calm subject carrying an obviously incompatible hybrid condition as if nothing is wrong."
Translate this into facial attitude, hair rhythm, costume architecture, material contradiction, anomaly color and hidden easter eggs.

R3/R4 real person references provide only the weak physical host anchor:
- Chinese adult male, mid-20s visual age;
- 182cm / 67kg visual proportion;
- tall, slim, long-limbed, non-muscular;
- natural shoulders, narrow waist;
- warm-neutral, somewhat deeper East Asian skin tone.
Do not copy the real face. Do not produce a blue-haired version of the real person.

CORE CHARACTER IDEA

"A high-resolution observer carrying low-resolution anomalies."

The character is calm, analytical and observant. He does not actively perform comedy. The more absurd the surrounding system becomes, the more restrained his reaction remains.

SILHOUETTE

Use a tall, narrow, vertical I-shaped silhouette rather than a heroic V-shaped body.
Natural/slightly narrow shoulders, long torso, long legs, light visual mass.
No oversized shoulders, armor, bulky coat or muscular build.

FACE

Create a newly designed young East Asian male character face, not a generic attractive CG male.

Face shape:
- slim and mildly elongated;
- soft rectangular upper structure;
- cheek area with slight horizontal support;
- lower face gradually narrows;
- relatively straight clean jaw;
- chin must not become sharp or V-shaped.

Eyes:
- adult East Asian male proportion;
- long and narrow;
- upper eyelid relatively flat/stable;
- only slight outer lift;
- deep blue-violet iris with near-black indigo outer ring;
- iris not oversized;
- calm analytical gaze, not fierce, romantic, sad or "CEO"-like;
- left eyelid very slightly lower than right.

Brows:
- natural and fairly straight;
- right brow tail very slightly higher;
- tiny natural sparse break near left inner-brow area;
- do not create a fashion shaved slit.

Nose:
- plausible young East Asian male structure;
- medium bridge height;
- slightly rounded tip;
- avoid hyper-perfect narrow AAA-game nose.

Mouth:
- slightly short in horizontal width;
- natural closed lips;
- restrained lip shape;
- right corner just slightly higher than left;
- no visible smile.
The fixed expression should read: "I noticed this is absurd; I do not need to perform that realization for you."

SKIN / GROOMING

- medium-value warm-neutral East Asian skin;
- slight healthy wheat/olive tendency is acceptable;
- no whitewashing;
- no glass-skin beauty-ad finish;
- controlled low T-zone shine;
- retain mild natural eye-socket / under-eye depth;
- natural low-saturation warm-brown-pink lips;
- high-end stylized-realism cinematic skin: believable volume, clean art direction, not pore-documentary photography and not plastic CG.

HAIR ARCHITECTURE

Do not create ordinary long blue hair. Design a repeatable silhouette using approximately five large stable hair masses before individual strands.

Overall:
- male medium-long hair around shoulder length;
- roots deep blue-gray;
- main body cool cyan-blue;
- pale ice-blue rim/highlight;
- inner tips may desaturate slightly toward gray-white;
- only a small number of loose strands.

Mass A:
- top body relatively close to head;
- major flow arcs from right-back toward left-front;
- avoid idol-volume puff.

Mass B:
- longest left-front framing lock;
- descends near left facial side to around jaw level.

Mass C:
- clearly shorter right-front framing lock;
- ends around brow-to-cheek region.

Mass D:
- on each upper side, a very subtle asymmetric outward/upward angular break in the silhouette;
- it may echo triangular rhythm but must NOT read as animal ears.

Mass E:
- stable rear body until nape;
- then divides into two larger flowing groups: one narrower/longer, one wider/shorter;
- in motion this may briefly echo tail-fin rhythm without becoming a literal fish-tail hairstyle.

PIXEL SUNGLASSES

Default position: on top of the head, eyes visible.

They must remain deliberately incompatible with the high-resolution character world:
- pure black/white block logic;
- obvious 90-degree corners;
- staircase / pixel edges;
- narrow horizontal overall form;
- do not smooth them into normal luxury eyewear;
- material can feel like a low-resolution digital asset physically instantiated.

Fixed intentional anomaly:
- right side terminates with one extra block/pixel.

Allow exactly one tiny dark-vermilion anomaly pixel.
Do not make red a visible main accent.

PRIMARY COSTUME

Design language: premium 2028 engineer / digital observer, not combat cyberpunk.

Inner layer:
- matte black/deep-charcoal half-high or high neck;
- minimal texture.

Outer jacket:
- cool gray-white / off-white technical textile;
- front length slightly below hip;
- rear length upper-mid thigh;
- narrow overall silhouette;
- natural shoulders;
- no armor.

Collar:
- asymmetric;
- left side relatively low/open;
- right side gradually rises toward jaw without touching face.

Structural lines:
- most seams/panels are straight, rational, engineered and geometric;
- one major seam beginning near right waist starts straight, then gradually becomes a soft organic curve and runs toward rear hem.

Rear hem:
- two offset panels;
- left panel narrower and slightly longer;
- right panel wider and slightly shorter;
- opening is an offset curved gap, not a symmetric swallow-tail V;
- static read must remain premium technical clothing, not fish costume.

Do not make the hidden lining/easter eggs visually dominant in this first concept. Only suggest that the inside may use a subtle darker structured textile.

PANTS
- charcoal / black slim-straight technical trousers;
- not skin-tight;
- avoid tactical pocket overload.

SHOES
- black minimal light technical low/mid shoe;
- no mech-boot silhouette.

COLOR HIERARCHY

Normal read should be overwhelmingly cool and restrained:
- black / deep gray;
- white / off-white;
- ice-cyan / cyan-blue;
- warm natural skin.

Warm anomaly color must stay below approximately 1% of the visible design.
Only the single tiny dark-vermilion pixel may be clearly present in this first render.

MATERIALS

- skin: soft organic stylized-realism, medium roughness;
- hair: semi-matte silky strands over large sculpted masses;
- jacket: fine woven matte technical textile;
- hardware: dark bead-blasted metal;
- sunglasses: unnaturally clean hard-edged pixel-solid material.

POSE / PERFORMANCE

Full body.
Natural standing pose.
Weight primarily on one leg; opposite knee relaxed.
Shoulders level.
Arms relaxed.
Head naturally biased roughly 2 degrees to the right.
Neutral deadpan observer expression.
No hands-on-hips, star pose, combat pose, fashion-runway pose or exaggerated emotion.

CAMERA / LIGHT

- approximately 75–85mm equivalent portrait perspective;
- eye-level camera;
- low perspective distortion;
- very simple neutral warm-gray studio background;
- large soft key from left-front-above;
- very light rim separation for cyan hair;
- no neon lighting;
- no technology city, servers, chips or story environment.

RENDER LANGUAGE

Stylized Realism Cinematic Character.
High-end designed 3D film character with believable human anatomy and premium materials.
Clearly art-directed as an original character.
Not photoreal human photography.
Not cosplay.
Not 2D anime.
Not Korean-idol photography.
Not a generic AAA handsome-male template.

STRICT EXCLUSIONS

No dog ears.
No dog nose/face.
No literal fish tail.
No scales on human skin.
No furry / kemonomimi.
No elf ears.
No sharp V chin.
No giant anime eyes.
No bulky muscles.
No armor.
No tactical combat loadout.
No neon-overloaded cyberpunk.
No large meme decals.
No Shiba image printed on the chest.
No fish image printed on the chest.
No comedy mascot expression.

OUTPUT CONTRACT

Generate exactly one character.
Show the complete full body.
Use a slight three-quarter body orientation while keeping the face clearly readable.
No text.
No design sheet.
No collage.
No environment.

The first render is only to validate the large-form character design: silhouette, face architecture, hair masses, pixel accessory logic and primary costume architecture. Do not force every hidden easter egg into this image.
```

## Review checklist after generation

Do not ask for a new random character immediately. First inspect:
1. silhouette: tall/narrow/recognizable?
2. face: memorable rather than generic handsome?
3. hair: large stable masses rather than blue wig / random strands?
4. pixel sunglasses: truly incompatible 8-bit object rather than normal eyewear?
5. costume: engineered order transitioning into organic flow without literal fish costume?
6. deadpan read: calm observer rather than cold CEO / idol / assassin?
7. commercial legibility: could this character coexist with technology, automotive and 3C brand work?

If large forms pass, continue with controlled edits on the same identity.
