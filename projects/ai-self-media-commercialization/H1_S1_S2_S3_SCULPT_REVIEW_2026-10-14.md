# H1 S1–S3 Sculptural Massing Review — 2026-10-14

Status: REVIEWED / S1+S3 HYBRID RECOMMENDED
Scope: H1 Face Primary Masses only
Inputs: S1 Source-Calibrated Neutral, S2 Structural Compression, S3 Anti-Generic Cheek/Jaw Rhythm clay/maquette front studies.

## Core finding

The sculptural pass is materially more useful than the earlier photoreal portrait passes because it exposes primary geometry without skin, grooming and beauty cues. However, none of S1–S3 should be locked wholesale.

Independent recommendation:
- retain S1 as the main envelope/orbit/quiet-expression base;
- reject S2 as a standalone direction;
- retain S3 only as a cheek→jaw→chin rhythm donor;
- create one controlled hybrid front sculpt `H1-S13-HYBRID` before any 3/4 validation.

## S1 — Source-Calibrated Neutral

Strengths:
- best same-source plausibility of the three;
- strongest horizontal / quiet eye read;
- useful moderately elongated envelope;
- lower face remains relatively light instead of becoming macho;
- compact rounded chin tendency is readable;
- most compatible with the desired calm observer baseline.

Weaknesses:
- cheek→jaw taper still drifts toward a conventional smooth attractive-male oval;
- lower-face rhythm is a little too generic and clean;
- the sculpt hair placeholder is irrelevant and must not be inherited.

Disposition: RETAIN as base geometry donor.

## S2 — Structural Compression

Strengths:
- adult male credibility;
- compact lower-face intent is partially present.

Weaknesses:
- model solved “compression” partly by broadening/regularizing the face rather than by the intended subtle vertical rhythm change;
- eye opening is not clearly reduced relative to S1 and in practice reads more open/neutral;
- brow/jaw system becomes more conventional and more generically masculine;
- lower face loses some of the source-derived lightness;
- insufficient gain in distinctiveness.

Disposition: REJECT as standalone base and do not use as a donor in the next hybrid.

## S3 — Anti-Generic Cheek/Jaw Rhythm

Strengths:
- best second-look distinction in the cheek→jaw→chin transition;
- cheek support is clearer without requiring a square jaw;
- remains fully human and commercially plausible;
- offers a useful way out of the generic handsome-male taper formula.

Weaknesses:
- eye/orbit system is less calm/source-like than S1;
- lower face can become too deliberately designed if copied wholesale;
- hair placeholder is highly stylized and irrelevant to H1;
- jaw/chin width must not be increased further.

Disposition: RETAIN only as lower-face transition donor.

## Hybrid target — H1-S13-HYBRID

Use:
- S1 for overall face envelope, forehead/temple proportion, orbital opening, calm horizontal eye system, nose/mouth placement tendency and overall soft-tissue massing;
- S3 only for the subtle cheek→jaw→compact-chin transition rhythm;
- creator certificate portrait only for same-source structural calibration, not appearance copying.

Do not use:
- S2 geometry;
- any sculpt hair placeholder;
- photoreal skin/hair;
- conventional handsomeness optimization;
- widened macho jaw;
- pointed/lengthened chin.

Desired hybrid correction:
1. preserve S1 face length/width and quiet orbital organization;
2. preserve S1 compact chin height;
3. increase/clarify lateral cheek support only slightly;
4. make the cheek→jaw transition less uniformly smooth than S1, borrowing S3’s rhythm but at lower intensity;
5. keep jaw moderate and light;
6. keep chin rounded-blunt, compact and not broad;
7. keep nose and mouth conservative and low-salience.
