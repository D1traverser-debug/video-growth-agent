# Character Design Bible — V1

## Design objective

Create one original male digital IP that can survive repeated AI-video generation, commercial-brand use, cinematic world-building and long-term identity accumulation.

The character must not collapse into any of these easy solutions:
- real creator + blue wig / blue hair;
- photoreal virtual human with meme glasses;
- generic handsome Asian AAA-game male;
- Korean-idol template;
- literal dog/fish hybrid;
- overloaded cyberpunk mascot.

## Core concept

> **A high-resolution observer carrying low-resolution anomalies.**

Chinese shorthand:

> **活在高精度世界里的低分辨率异常观察者。**

Narrative function:
- he enters precise systems;
- identifies mechanisms, incentives, bugs and absurdity;
- the more absurd the world becomes, the less performative his reaction becomes.

This is not merely a visual mascot. It is a narrative instrument for technology, business/capital and internet-culture stories.

## Reference-source responsibilities

### Pixel avatar — first-read brand DNA
Carries:
- ice-cyan / cyan-blue hair;
- deep blue-violet irises;
- black-and-white pixel sunglasses;
- white / ice-blue / black palette;
- 8-bit, geek, digital-native and light meme lineage.

It defines **recognition**, not facial anatomy.

### Shiba-carp meme — motif / hybrid / easter-egg / performance grammar
Do **not** copy dog ears, dog face, fish tail or animal anatomy.

Extract these deeper principles:
1. impossible hybrid treated as normal;
2. deadpan subject inside absurdity;
3. organic flowing system colliding with rigid unrelated system;
4. Shiba triangular rhythm;
5. carp split-flow / scale / water motifs;
6. red-orange-gold anomaly color hidden inside a predominantly cool identity.

### Real creator — weak host anchor only
Use only:
- young Chinese adult male;
- roughly mid-20s visual age;
- 182cm / 67kg tall-slim archetype;
- long limbs, natural shoulders, narrow waist, non-muscular frame;
- warm-neutral, somewhat deeper East Asian skin tone;
- analytical / engineering presence.

Do not treat the real face as the target face.

## Audience-read hierarchy

### First read — 0.5 to 2 seconds
Read:
- tall/slim male;
- ice-cyan hair silhouette;
- black/white pixel bar on head;
- narrow vertical outfit silhouette;
- restrained cool palette.

Do **not** expose all meme references immediately.

### Second read — 2 to 10 seconds
Read:
- unusual but controlled facial asymmetry;
- calm observational gaze;
- nonstandard hair architecture;
- costume structure that shifts from rational geometry to softer organic flow.

### Third read / fandom discovery
Discover:
- single red anomaly pixel;
- pixel-to-scale interior textile motif;
- split-tail hem movement;
- 5×5 abstract Shiba symbol inside sunglass arm;
- subtle ripple motif;
- tiny internal error label / mismatch code.

## Silhouette

Overall silhouette: **vertical I-form**, not heroic V-form.

- natural/slightly narrow shoulder line;
- long torso and long legs;
- no oversized shoulder armor;
- no bulky coat swallowing the frame;
- coat remains narrow through torso;
- visual order begins to split only near lower hem.

The body should remain recognizably 182/67 in visual proportion: tall, slim, light, long-limbed.

## Face architecture

Design a memorable character face, not an optimized handsome template.

### Face shape
- slim, mildly elongated;
- soft-rectangular upper structure;
- cheek area retains slight horizontal support;
- lower face narrows gradually;
- jawline relatively straight and clean;
- chin short enough to avoid a V-face / idol look;
- no razor-sharp pointed chin.

### Nose
- medium bridge height;
- avoid narrow hyper-perfect CG nose;
- tip slightly rounded;
- preserve plausible East Asian male structure.

### Mouth
- slightly shorter horizontal width;
- natural closed mouth;
- restrained lip definition;
- right corner very slightly higher than left;
- no obvious smile.

Fixed read:
> "I noticed that this is absurd; I do not need to perform that realization for you."

### Eyes
- adult East Asian male proportion;
- long and narrow;
- upper eyelid comparatively flat/stable;
- only slight outer lift;
- iris not oversized;
- deep blue-violet iris with near-black indigo outer ring;
- blue/violet becomes noticeable on closer viewing rather than screaming immediately.

Micro-asymmetry:
- left eyelid very slightly lower than right.

### Brows
- relatively straight and natural;
- right brow tail very slightly higher;
- tiny natural sparse break near left inner-brow region;
- not a shaved fashion slit;
- no obvious performed eyebrow raise.

## Grooming / skin

- medium-value warm-neutral East Asian skin tone;
- slight wheat/olive tendency acceptable;
- do not whitewash;
- no glass-skin beauty-ad look;
- T-zone controlled, low shine;
- retain mild natural eye-socket / under-eye depth;
- lips natural low-saturation warm brown-pink;
- skin is stylized cinematic realism: believable volume, clean art direction, not pore-documentary photorealism and not plastic CG.

## Hair architecture

Hair must be designed as large stable masses before individual strands.

Target: male medium-long hair, around shoulder length, **5 primary masses**.

### Mass A — top body
- relatively close to head, not idol-volume puff;
- main flow arcs from right-back toward left-front;
- keeps head silhouette elongated rather than oversized.

### Mass B — left-front framing lock
- longest front lock;
- descends along left facial side;
- reaches around jaw level.

### Mass C — right-front framing lock
- clearly shorter than B;
- ends between brow/cheek region;
- locks permanent left-right asymmetry.

### Mass D — upper side silhouette breaks
- one subtle upward/outward angular turn on each upper side;
- asymmetric heights;
- abstract echo of Shiba triangular rhythm;
- must **not** read as animal ears.

### Mass E — rear split-flow
- stable rear body until nape;
- then divides into two larger flowing groups;
- one narrower/longer, one wider/shorter;
- in motion, the split may briefly echo tail-fin rhythm;
- must never become a literal fish-tail hairstyle.

Color hierarchy:
- roots: deep blue-gray;
- main body: cool cyan-blue;
- rim/highlights: pale ice-blue;
- inner tips may desaturate slightly toward gray-white.

Avoid:
- uniform saturated cyan;
- random flying strands;
- female princess hair;
- salon-fashion blue-dye realism.

## Signature accessory — pixel sunglasses

The sunglasses must remain deliberately **incompatible** with the high-resolution character world.

Do not smooth them into ordinary premium eyewear.

Design rules:
- black base;
- white block pattern;
- obvious 90° corners;
- staircase/pixel edges;
- narrow horizontal overall form;
- material can feel like a digital asset physically instantiated;
- default placement on top of head, eyes visible.

Intentional fixed error:
- right side ends with one extra pixel/block.

Hidden detail:
- inside left temple/arm: tiny 5×5 abstract Shiba icon using two ear points, two eye points and one nose point.

Anomaly indicator:
- exactly one tiny dark vermilion/red pixel.

## Costume architecture

Design language:
> **2028 premium engineer / digital observer**, not 2088 combat cyberpunk.

### Inner layer
- black/deep-charcoal matte half-high or high neck;
- minimal texture;
- no decorative tech clutter.

### Outer layer
- cool off-white / gray-white technical jacket;
- front length to slightly below hip;
- rear length to upper-mid thigh;
- narrow overall silhouette;
- natural shoulders;
- no armor.

### Collar
Asymmetric:
- left side relatively low/open;
- right side gradually rises toward jaw without touching face.

### Structural-line logic
Most seams/panels:
- straight;
- rational;
- engineered;
- geometric.

One major line beginning near right waist:
- starts straight;
- slowly turns into a soft organic curve;
- travels toward rear hem.

This is the central **hybrid-system fusion**: engineering order becomes biological flow without literal animal/fish graphics.

### Rear hem
Two panels:
- left: narrower and slightly longer;
- right: wider and slightly shorter;
- gap is an offset curved opening, not a conventional symmetric swallow-tail V.

Static read: premium technical garment.  
Motion read: occasional abstract tail-fin expansion rhythm.

### Interior textile / Pixel→Scale motif
Interior only:
- fine gray-blue-black jacquard / technical weave;
- repeated units transition visually from squarer pixel geometry at top into softened rhombus/scale-like lower curves;
- from distance reads as technical fabric;
- close viewing reveals biological-scale rhythm.

This pattern is intended as a reusable channel motif for UI, transitions, merchandise and background systems.

### Pants
- charcoal / black;
- slim-straight technical trousers;
- not skin-tight;
- do not overload with tactical pockets.

### Shoes
- black, light, minimal technical low/mid shoe;
- thin/controlled sole;
- rear outsole may hide a very shallow twin-split groove as a deep easter egg.

## Color system

Approximate normal-state balance:
- ~42% black/deep gray;
- ~28% white/off-white;
- ~22% ice-cyan / cyan-blue;
- ~7% warm natural skin;
- <1% anomaly warm color.

Anomaly warm color:
- dark vermilion / red-orange;
- occasional muted gold only in hidden interior detail.

Narrative meaning of warm anomaly color:
- bug;
- risk;
- FOMO;
- capital frenzy;
- hallucination;
- system instability;
- internet loss of control.

The character is not a red-blue dual-color hero. Warm anomalies stay rare.

## Material hierarchy

- skin: soft organic stylized-realism, medium roughness;
- hair: semi-matte silky surface on large sculpted masses;
- outerwear: fine woven matte technical textile;
- lining: subtle low-sheen jacquard;
- hardware: dark bead-blasted metal;
- pixel sunglasses: unusually clean hard-edged pixel-solid material that resists being visually normalized into the rest of the world.

## Performance DNA

Default state: **Neutral Deadpan Observer**.

Expression amplitude roughly 60% of an ordinary animated/video-presenter character.

### Default stance
- weight mainly on one leg;
- opposite knee relaxed;
- shoulders level;
- arms relaxed;
- no hands-on-hips presenter pose.

### Head signature
Natural head bias: roughly 2–3° to the right.

When something becomes especially absurd:
- head tilts a few additional degrees;
- pauses briefly;
- returns;
- no wide eyes / open mouth / exaggerated reaction.

This is the Shiba-derived performance signature.

### Gesture vocabulary
Prefer:
- single index finger;
- two-finger indication;
- one open palm;
- pointing at a specific mechanism/system.

Avoid constant presenter gesticulation.

### Movement
- long-stride walk consistent with tall frame;
- upper body comparatively stable;
- small arm swing;
- costume hem and rear hair carry more motion than torso.

## Cinematography identity

### Canon identity reference
- ~85mm equivalent;
- eye-level camera;
- low perspective distortion;
- neutral background;
- large soft source;
- purpose: identity truth, not drama.

### Entering systems
- ~35–50mm equivalent;
- environment becomes larger; character becomes smaller;
- purpose: human/observer inside complex system.

### Absurd-system staging
Prefer a stable camera while the world becomes chaotic around him.

The comedy should come from contrast:
- environment goes insane;
- character does not perform insanity.

## Easter-egg tiers

### Tier 1 — signature, eventually obvious
- pixel sunglasses;
- one anomaly red pixel;
- asymmetric hair masses;
- split-flow hem.

### Tier 2 — repeat viewers discover
- Pixel→Scale lining;
- hidden ripple/water motif;
- subtle structural line changing from geometric to organic;
- asymmetrical eye/brow/mouth micro-features.

### Tier 3 — deep callbacks
- 5×5 hidden Shiba symbol;
- tiny internal mismatch/error code;
- outsole split groove;
- recurring ripple UI;
- same anomaly red point resurfacing in narrative systems.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

Do not generate:
- dog ears;
- dog nose/face;
- literal fish tail;
- scales on human skin;
- furry/kemonomimi;
- elf ears;
- generic Korean-idol male;
- sharp V chin;
- anime giant eyes;
- bulky muscle frame;
- armor / tactical combat loadout;
- neon-overloaded cyberpunk;
- giant meme decals;
- Shiba image printed on chest;
- fish graphics printed on chest;
- obvious joke mascot.

## Current production rule

The first new concept image should validate **large forms only**:
- overall silhouette;
- face architecture;
- hair masses;
- sunglasses logic;
- primary costume architecture;
- restrained motif fusion.

Do not force every easter egg into the first render. After the large-form concept passes, use controlled editing to add/refine secondary and tertiary details without changing identity.
