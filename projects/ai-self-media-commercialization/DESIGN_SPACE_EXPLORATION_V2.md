# Character Design Space Exploration — V2

Status: ACTIVE_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_LOCKED
Scope: project-local only
Purpose: actively explore the plausible character/fashion/styling design space before locking Character Design Bible V2. This file is not user-approval evidence and does not auto-lock a direction.

## Governance rule

Do not immediately accept a user suggestion as design truth.

For every material design decision:
1. identify the design problem;
2. generate a representative option space across different visual systems;
3. compare options against the project objective;
4. state an independent recommendation and the rejected trade-offs;
5. persist the recommendation as `PROPOSED` until explicitly adopted through project decisions.

Literal enumeration of every possible design is impossible because the design space is effectively unbounded. The operational substitute is a representative exhaustive sweep across the major independent axes: body archetype, face architecture, hair silhouette, tailoring/drape system, styling contradiction, accessory scale, performance and presentation.

## Research distillation

### Fashion is a total identity system, not clothing decoration
Museum and runway references show that fashion can communicate culture, identity, politics and character through silhouette, proportion, tailoring, styling and staging. A runway look should therefore be judged as a complete character proposition rather than as an outfit placed on a pre-existing face.

### Silhouette and proportion are first-order
FIT design principles emphasize balance, proportion, emphasis and rhythm. Garment evaluation treats silhouette/proportion as core quality criteria. This project therefore evaluates the whole-body read before pockets, zippers, trims or textile easter eggs.

### Tailoring is controlled manipulation of body line
The Met's tailoring material demonstrates that tailoring can create very different identities by changing shoulder, neck, torso and leg relationships. We are not constrained to the creator's literal everyday proportions; the real body is an anchor, not a prison.

### Deconstruction must be built on construction
V&A material on tailoring/deconstruction and Balenciaga shows that deliberate asymmetry or apparent simplicity only works when construction, proportion and material behavior are coherent first. Random asymmetry is not avant-garde design.

### Runway concept can precede garment details
McQueen's process often began with a concept/runway idea and storyboard before garment realization. Runway/show styling, hair, makeup, movement and environment can all carry the same character thesis.

### Productive design strategies available to this project
From museum/runway research, useful strategies include:
- architectural minimalism;
- precise tailoring;
- deconstruction;
- asymmetry/imbalance;
- fusion of incompatible systems;
- elimination/reduction;
- exaggerated proportion;
- soft drape versus hard tailoring;
- dandyism / deliberate self-fashioning;
- anti-fashion / subversion;
- total-look theatricality;
- conceptual staging.

## Design axes to sweep

### A. Physical presence
A1 — realistic tall-slim adult
A2 — attenuated runway figure: slightly longer neck/legs, controlled elegance
A3 — compact upper body + long legs: intellectual/dandy emphasis
A4 — broader shoulder + narrow pelvis: authority, but risks generic heroism
A5 — softer narrow shoulder + visible hip/pelvis: androgynous editorial quality
A6 — angular bony frame: severe/ascetic, risks sickly read

### B. Face architecture
B1 — natural analytical Chinese male, understated planes
B2 — editorial bony face, strong cheek/jaw planes
B3 — compact blunt face with narrow eyes, deadpan/strange charisma
B4 — elegant dandy face, refined but risks idol/beauty drift
B5 — slightly uncanny clean face, minimal affect, risks synthetic/alien read

### C. Hair architecture
C1 — medium architectural crop, large cyan planes, low strand noise
C2 — jaw/neck-length asymmetrical sculpted hair
C3 — shoulder-length split-flow hair, strongest connection to original avatar but highest anime risk
C4 — short back + one long asymmetric lock, strongest commercial stability
C5 — controlled wet/slicked runway hair with one geometric cyan interruption, strongest fashion read but weaker avatar continuity

### D. Clothing system
D1 — precision contemporary tailoring
D2 — deconstructed tailoring
D3 — sculptural minimalism / architectural garment
D4 — luxury workwear / atelier-engineer
D5 — soft draped monochrome layers
D6 — neo-dandy formalwear
D7 — anti-fashion / subversive street tailoring

### E. Contradiction system

E2 — hard tailored torso + soft organic lower drape
E3 — symmetric base + one major asymmetric construction rupture
E4 — matte low-information body + one high-information digital object

### F. Performance/staging

## Representative design directions

### Direction 1 — Precision Observer / 精密观察者
System:
- A2 attenuated but believable body;
- B1 natural analytical face;
- C4 short-back + one long cyan lock;
- D1 precision tailoring;
- E1 classic tailoring + pixel object;
- F1 documentary observer.

Strengths:
- highly commercial;
- credible for tech/business;
- easy to dress across sponsors;
- AI consistency relatively strong.

Weaknesses:
- risks becoming tasteful but forgettable;
- meme/internet-culture layer too dependent on accessory.

Assessment: strong baseline, insufficient as final identity by itself.

### Direction 2 — Deconstructed Analyst / 解构分析者
System:
- A1/A2 tall-slim;
- B3 compact/blunt deadpan face;
- C2 asymmetrical sculpted hair;
- D2 deconstructed tailoring;
- E3 one structural rupture;
- F3 detached archivist.

Strengths:
- visual contradiction can be built into the garment rather than pasted on;
- intellectually aligned with 'disassembling systems';
- distinctive without literal sci-fi styling.

Weaknesses:
- easy to become art-school costume if deconstruction is too loud;
- requires excellent proportion control.

Assessment: strong candidate.

### Direction 3 — Sculptural Minimalist / 建筑极简体

- B5 clean uncanny face;

Strengths:

Weaknesses:

Assessment: useful source, not recommended as sole direction.

### Direction 5 — Neo-Dandy / 新绅士

## Independent recommendation

Do not choose a single existing archetype literally.

Recommended synthesis, provisional name:

> **Glitch Dandy / 异常绅士**

This is not 'a fashionable man'. It is a long-term character system built from:
- 40% Precision Observer / contemporary tailoring credibility;
- 25% Deconstructed Analyst / one real structural rupture;
- 20% Neo-Dandy / deliberate self-fashioning and iconic proportion;
- 10% Soft Drape Archivist / movement and organic counter-rhythm;
- 5% Anti-Fashion Internet Native / controlled awkwardness and meme contamination.

Explicitly exclude Techwear as a base chassis.

## Proposed emotional read

First read:
- composed;
- intelligent;
- adult;
- elegant without trying to look expensive;
- physically light but not weak.

Second read:
- something in the proportions/construction is deliberately 'off';
- the pixel object is too crude for the rest of the look;
- asymmetry is structural, not decorative.

Long-term read:
- he is not a teacher, CEO, game hero or fashion model;
- he is a self-possessed observer who can enter technology, capital and internet absurdity without changing identity.

## Proposed macro-form direction for V2

### Body
- do not simply reproduce 182/67;
- use an attenuated but believable adult male body;
- roughly 7.8–8.1 heads, not extreme 9-head fashion illustration;
- neck slightly longer than average but not swan-like;
- shoulders natural, gently sloped, not narrow to the point of fragility;
- ribcage slim but present;
- pelvis visually stable;
- long legs with a high but not artificial waist;
- hands slightly expressive/long-fingered, feet not oversized.

Goal: 'editorial intelligence', not 'runway mannequin'.

### Face
Leading proposal: B3 + B1 hybrid.
- broader/blunter primary face mass than V1;
- cheek support stronger;
- chin compact and not pointed;
- narrower eyes placed with calm horizontal emphasis;
- nose and mouth natural rather than beauty-optimized;
- retain warmth and human specificity;
- attractiveness comes from coherence and presence, not perfect features.

### Hair
Leading proposal: C4/C2 hybrid.
- shorter and more architectural than V1;
- back length around upper neck, not shoulder-length anime hair;
- one longer asymmetric front/side lock preserves avatar lineage;
- 3–4 major masses, not 20 strands;
- cyan is a shape/color block first, hair texture second.

### Garment chassis
Leading proposal: contemporary/deconstructed tailoring, not techwear.
- one short/medium tailored outer layer with a strong front proportion;
- controlled shoulder and high waist relation;
- straight or slightly wide long trouser rather than cargo/slim tactical pants;
- one construction rupture that turns from strict tailoring into soft drape/asymmetric panel;
- no visible utility pockets unless truly functional to the design;
- no decorative piping/zippers as identity.

### Pixel sunglasses
- treat as a graphic object with enough scale to interrupt the head silhouette;
- should read at thumbnail scale;
- the crude 8-bit geometry is intentional and should not be luxury-normalized.

### Shiba-carp fusion
Do not encode it first as ears/scales/tail.

Macro:
- 'precise refined human + one visibly incompatible visual rule'.

Meso:
- garment/hair construction transitions from disciplined tailoring/geometry into a softer split-flow rhythm.

Micro:
- later: hidden symbol, anomaly pixel, textile or code.

## Runway / campaign system

The canon character should support multiple presentation modes without changing identity:
1. CANON — neutral design truth;
2. EDITORIAL — fashion-forward controlled styling;
3. CINEMATIC — story/world integration;
4. SURREAL — special campaign/runway visual where the environment becomes the anomaly;
5. COMMERCIAL — sponsor-compatible wardrobe translation.

The base canon should not be as theatrical as the most extreme runway version. Runway logic is a source of identity and proportion, not a requirement that every video look like a catwalk.
