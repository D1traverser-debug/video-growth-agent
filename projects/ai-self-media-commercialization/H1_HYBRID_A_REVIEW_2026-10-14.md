# H1 Hybrid A Front Review — 2026-10-14

Status: REVIEWED / FAIL AS FACE LOCK
Scope: H1 Face Primary Masses only
Inputs: one GPT-generated source-calibrated Hybrid A front-view study + creator certificate portrait.

## Core finding

Hybrid A is not suitable for H1 lock.

The source-calibration pass pulled the output closer to the creator's general facial organization, but the rendering/domain itself caused a new failure mode: the result became an ordinary photoreal East-Asian male portrait rather than a designed Human Core. The face is plausible, but not sufficiently character-designed or structurally distinctive.

The main problem is no longer only the facial proportions. GPT is now satisfying the task through realistic portrait synthesis and conventional male-attractiveness priors instead of exposing and controlling primary masses.

## What improved

Compared with the text-only F1–F5 set:
- brow/eye placement is somewhat softer and closer to the source;
- eye orientation is more horizontal and less fashion-model sharpened;
- nose and mouth are less idealized than the more polished F1–F5 outputs;
- cheek support + compact lower-face relation is closer to the intended source-derived organization;
- the result no longer has the severe F3-style editorial/macho drift.

## What still fails

1. **Too photoreal / too ordinary**
   - reads like a plausible studio portrait of a real young man;
   - does not read as a deliberate character-architecture study;
   - styling neutrality did not remove the portrait-generation prior.

2. **Generic attractive-male basin remains**
   - brow mass still slightly heavier and more designed than the source;
   - jaw/chin still feel conventionally masculine and balanced rather than uniquely constructed;
   - nose is still cleaner/straighter than the creator source tendency;
   - mouth remains more polished and symmetrical than desired.

3. **Insufficient separation from creator / insufficient independent identity**
   - because the certificate portrait was directly supplied, the model moved toward a plausible transformed version of the creator rather than a new architecture with same-source resonance;
   - this is neither a useful digital clone nor a sufficiently independent IP face.

4. **Primary-mass differences are hidden by skin/hair realism**
   - skin texture, hair, eyes and photographic realism dominate perception;
   - the review cannot cleanly isolate skull envelope, zygomatic width, jaw taper and chin mass.

## Decision

Do NOT continue by editing this photoreal portrait.
Do NOT generate a 3/4 version of Hybrid A in the same representation domain.
Do NOT treat this image as a new face reference for later stages.

Hybrid A front = FAIL AS LOCK, but useful as evidence that direct certificate-photo calibration in a photoreal portrait domain is the wrong representation for H1.

## Method correction — proposed next step

Insert an intermediate sculptural-massing pass before any more portrait rendering.

### H1-SCULPT-A / neutral clay maquette

Use the certificate portrait only as structural reference, but request a neutral monochrome clay/maquette head instead of a human portrait:
- matte gray clay material;
- no realistic skin;
- no eyelashes/eyebrow hair detail;
- no iris color;
- no realistic hair; use a very simple fitted skull-cap / blocked hair mass or bald study;
- no clothing/fashion identity;
- soft neutral studio light;
- strict front view first;
- emphasize skull envelope, brow plane, orbital opening, cheek width, maxilla, jaw taper and chin mass.

Reason:
Removing photographic skin/hair/beauty cues should force GPT to solve the geometry as form rather than as an attractive portrait.

### Comparison strategy

Do not generate five full portraits again.
Generate 3 low-information clay variants around the same source-derived base:
- S1 Source-Calibrated Neutral — closest plausible source structure;
- S2 Structural Compression — slightly more compact lower-face/chin rhythm + calm eye opening;
- S3 Anti-Generic Cheek/Jaw Rhythm — stronger cheek support + less conventional cheek→jaw→compact-chin transition without broadening jaw.

All non-geometry variables identical.

After review:
- either select one sculpt geometry;
- or deliberately hybridize two sculpt variants;
- only then reintroduce stylized skin/hair in a separate representation pass.
