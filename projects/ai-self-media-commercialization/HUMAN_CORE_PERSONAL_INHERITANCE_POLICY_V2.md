# Human Core Personal Inheritance Policy V2

Status: ACTIVE DESIGN POLICY
Updated: 2026-10-14
Scope: creator-derived facial/body traits for original digital IP Human Core.

## Decision

The creator does NOT need to be conventionally handsome for real-person traits to be useful.

The project should not preserve the creator wholesale and should not erase the creator wholesale. It should use **selective structural inheritance**:

1. preserve a small set of source-specific proportional relationships that create same-source resonance;
2. redesign weak/generic/noisy traits that do not help recognizability, long-term IP identity, motion stability or commercial expression;
3. do not replace everything with standard beauty-model priors;
4. do not treat every visible imperfection as a required identity marker;
5. optimize for recognizability, character coherence and long-term extensibility rather than maximum attractiveness or maximum likeness.

## Why

A fully copied face risks becoming a digital double / dyed-hair cosplay version of the creator.
A fully replaced face loses the future bridge between creator and IP and tends to collapse toward generic handsome-male priors.
The useful middle is a **same-source original**: visibly designed, not a clone, but carrying enough structural ancestry that creator and IP feel related.

## Three-tier inheritance model

### Tier A — Preserve / calibrate strongly
Use relationships, not surface copying.

Current candidate anchors from the certificate portrait:
- overall face width/length tendency;
- horizontal brow-eye organization;
- eye spacing / orbital placement tendency;
- meaningful cheek support;
- compact mouth-to-chin relation;
- compact rounded/blunt chin tendency;
- natural medium nose volume rather than hyper-refined narrow CG nose;
- adult Chinese male baseline.

These are valuable because they resist generic beauty-template substitution while remaining compatible with redesign.

### Tier B — Transform deliberately
May be stylized or rebalanced to create stronger IP identity:
- cheek→jaw→chin rhythm;
- eye aperture;
- brow mass / brow thickness;
- lower-face vertical rhythm;
- soft-tissue fullness;
- head/face plane clarity;
- asymmetry level;
- later hairline / hair silhouette.

Any transformation must be judged in grayscale / no-brand conditions before styling is allowed to carry identity.

### Tier C — Do not preserve as identity
Do not treat the following as mandatory likeness anchors:
- retouching / whitening / makeup from the certificate photo;
- current haircut;
- skin blemishes or transient texture;
- suit / photo styling;
- camera-specific distortions;
- micro-asymmetries that do not survive angle or motion;
- traits preserved only because they are visible in one photograph;
- arbitrary imperfections that reduce stability without adding identity.

## Attractiveness policy

Conventional handsomeness is NOT the optimization target.
The project may perform controlled idealization, but only when it improves one of:
- structural coherence;
- memorability;
- emotional readability;
- AI-video stability;
- sponsor/commercial adaptability;
- same-source resonance.

Do not idealize by default into:
- K-idol face;
- AAA-game male face;
- luxury-model face;
- sharp V-jaw;
- hyper-narrow high nose;
- heroic brow;
- sculpted sensual lips.

A designed face may be more attractive than the creator, less attractive by conventional standards, or simply different. The criterion is whether it is **more characterful and more ownable**.

## Practical target

Desired relationship:

> creator and IP are not the same person, but viewers can plausibly feel that the IP could have evolved from the creator's structural ancestry.

The IP must remain recognizable when:
- hair is dark/plain;
- eyes are dark;
- pixel glasses are removed;
- clothing is neutral;
- image is grayscale;
- angle changes.

## Current H1 implication

During `H1_SCULPTURAL_MASSING_V2`, use the certificate portrait only to calibrate Tier-A structural anchors. S1/S2/S3 sculpt variants are free to redesign Tier-B relationships. Do not reintroduce beauty/styling cues until geometry is selected.
