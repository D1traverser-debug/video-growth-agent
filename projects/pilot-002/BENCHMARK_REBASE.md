# Pilot 002 — Moving Benchmark Rebase

Purpose: calibrate the next sample against moving, finished work rather than still-image taste alone. These references are measuring instruments, not templates to clone.

## B1 — BUCK / Nothing, Playground
Source: https://buck.co/work/nothing-playground
Role: **craft / brand-world benchmark**

Why it matters:
- an AI product launch is expressed as a full visual world rather than a software walkthrough;
- strict brand DNA (grid, dots, type, transparency) is pushed into tactile texture and human/nature footage;
- graphic precision and organic texture coexist;
- the studio explicitly built a large library of styleframes, art-direction studies and motion tests, many of which were rejected before the final film.

Observable traits to calibrate:
1. each shot has one dominant visual event;
2. brand primitives transform across media rather than repeating one UI motif;
3. texture, color and footage prevent the film from reading as a dashboard;
4. transitions feel born from the design system;
5. exploration is abundant but final language is coherent.

Pilot 002 target:
- the Agent/App topic must gain a tactile/human contrast instead of remaining glass UI for the whole piece;
- the sample must show at least one material or media change that could not be described as “cards moving.”

Do not copy: Nothing’s proprietary dot/grid/typographic identity or exact compositions.

## B2 — BUCK / Microsoft Azure AI Platform
Source: https://buck.co/work/microsoft-azure
Role: **3D/material/metaphor benchmark**

Why it matters:
- tactile CGI and metaphors of light/glass carry abstract AI concepts;
- 3D, 2D, editorial, color, music and sound are treated as one production system;
- the object/material world feels physically authored, not like CSS glassmorphism.

Observable traits to calibrate:
1. glass/light have physical behavior, scale and photographic staging;
2. abstract concepts resolve through material metaphor;
3. depth and lighting create hierarchy before text does;
4. close/mid/wide scale changes make the film breathe;
5. audio/editorial are first-class craft roles.

Pilot 002 target:
- if “permission / agent routing / background services” are visualized materially, they must have believable weight, lighting, contact and camera relation;
- fake glass cards on a flat background are below benchmark.

Do not copy: Azure’s exact glass/light motifs, brand palette or shot arrangements.

## B3 — TAQA AI Agent System / Ahmed Asaad
Source: https://asaad.art/work/taqa-ai-agent-system
Video: https://vimeo.com/1232910425
Role: **same-problem explanation benchmark**

Why it matters:
- the exact challenge is turning a multi-agent workflow into something immediately understandable without software-documentation aesthetics;
- every agent becomes a mechanically believable gear; the system is understood as the machine assembles;
- one visual metaphor persists while scale, spacing, reflections and composition expand.

Observable traits to calibrate:
1. complexity is progressively revealed rather than announced as a finished flowchart;
2. metaphor has physical causality: parts actually connect and operate;
3. negative space keeps dense logic readable;
4. final payoff is the complete machine, not a final title card;
5. motion and explanation are the same action.

Pilot 002 target:
- foreground app interaction → background agent/service system must become one causally understandable transformation;
- every connection shown must communicate mechanism, not decoration.

Do not copy: glass gear metaphor, exact blue lighting, gear layouts or transitions.

## B4 — Ordinary Folk / Google Gemini + Google Montage / AI Mode work
Sources:
- https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/project/gemini
- https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/project/google-montage
Role: **motion/typography/explanation benchmark**

Why it matters:
- meaningful typography supports product capability rather than turning into generic kinetic text;
- a restrained brand language still achieves high visual reset frequency;
- sound and animation are conceived together from early stages in Ordinary Folk’s published process.

Observable traits to calibrate:
1. every text move has semantic intent;
2. simple geometry is saved by excellent timing, composition and transformation logic, not glow;
3. motion personality stays consistent across many micro-scenes;
4. high information density does not require dashboard framing;
5. sound, score and VO are planned throughout, not attached at export.

Pilot 002 target:
- code motion remains useful for type/data/UI overlays, but may not carry the cinematic world by itself;
- the sample needs a clear picture-sound relationship at the key reveal.

Do not copy: Google’s brand shapes, Gemini gradient identity, exact typography or sequences.

# Side-by-side failure test for the next sample

At normal playback speed, the next 5–12s sample must not immediately lose to these references on all of the following:

- dominant focal event;
- material/asset richness;
- spatial/camera intention;
- transformation causality;
- motion timing and holds;
- picture/sound cohesion;
- memorable hero moment;
- absence of generic SaaS / AI-template feel.

If a benchmark wins immediately in a hard dimension, score that dimension below 4 and reject the sample. “Different style” is not a defense for lower craft.
