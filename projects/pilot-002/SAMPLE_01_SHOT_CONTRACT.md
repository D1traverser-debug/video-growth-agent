# Pilot 002 — Sample 01 Audiovisual Shot Contract

Status: PRODUCTION / VISUAL_PREVIEW CANDIDATE  
Stage: LOOKDEV → VISUAL_PREVIEW  
Target: 10.22 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, vertical 9:16  
Narrative source timing: 00:15.76–00:25.98 from the validated V4 narration  
Purpose: test the hardest transition from foreground app-by-app human operation into a deeper agent/service world.

## 1. Hard design thesis

The opening must feel human, tactile and familiar. The ending must feel spatially deeper, causally understandable and materially different.

This is **not** a software walkthrough, flowchart, glass-card UI, dashboard, neon-AI tunnel, or moving-slide sequence.

The mechanism should read while muted: many visible manual surface actions collapse into one delegated background system.

## 2. Audio / narration authority

Representative narration:

> 以前你要找邮件、看文件、查资料、拉表、做 PPT、再回复。  
> Agent 想把这些步骤藏到后台。  
> 你只说：把它准备好。

Reuse the already validated narration audio from the historical V4 only as an audio source; **do not reuse V4 picture or art-direction authority**.

Sound intent:
- opening: quiet real desk/room tone, fingertip/tap/keyboard micro-SFX;
- transition: one decisive tactile click → short vacuum/suction pull + low sub hit;
- backend reveal: restrained mechanical/electrical ambience with discrete routing ticks;
- final line: ambience opens and settles; one resolved tonal hit when the completed artifact arrives.

No generic heroic trailer riser. No constant whoosh bed.

## 3. Shot structure

### SAMP01-A — HUMAN SURFACE / 0.00–2.20 s

**Hero event:** a realistic hand rapidly switches between familiar work surfaces on a phone/laptop workspace; each action is clearly manual and slightly tedious.

Composition:
- extreme close / macro-biased crop;
- real or licensed photographic/video material, shallow depth, visible skin/device/workspace texture;
- the device is not presented as a clean UI mockup floating in space;
- one dominant focal point: fingertip + active screen region.

Motion:
- subtle handheld/parallax or real camera drift;
- 2–3 crisp interaction beats, not a montage of branded app logos;
- first visible change before 0.5 s.

Typography: none, or at most one tiny semantic label; narration carries the list.

### SAMP01-B — SURFACE BREAK / 2.20–5.40 s

**Causal transition:** on the decisive interaction, the visible screen surface splits into thin functional layers in Z-depth. Content does not become floating cards; instead, work objects become distinct physical tokens/materials and are pulled through the device plane.

Required spatial change:
- camera crosses the screen/device plane or performs an equivalent unmistakable depth transition;
- foreground human/workspace falls away and cannot still dominate the frame;
- at least one start/end state is intentionally anchored: start = fingertip/device surface; end = fully inside service space.

Physical behavior:
- mail/message content → thin paper-like strip or envelope/token;
- file/context → translucent indexed sheet or document block;
- table/data → compact grid/data ribbon;
- presentation/output → bound frame/object;
- each object has weight, occlusion and path; no hovering icon soup.

Sound: tactile click, brief suction/pull, low-frequency impact at plane crossing.

### SAMP01-C — BACKGROUND AGENT WORLD / 5.40–10.22 s

**Mechanism reveal:** the camera is now inside a deeper service environment. Distinct work tokens route through a small number of materially believable stations and converge into one delegated task/result.

Visual logic:
- 3–4 service stations max; each has a distinct role and material cue rather than a logo;
- paths activate because objects arrive; connections are causal, not decorative;
- central orchestration should be understood through behavior before any label;
- avoid literal robot brain, glowing AI orb, circuit-board cliché, infinity tunnel and generic hologram HUD.

Payoff:
- the system resolves into one completed market-update artifact/object that moves toward camera;
- optional minimal type only at the final beat: `把它准备好` or `一次委托`;
- end frame must be compositionally and materially unmistakable from the opening human/device frame.

Sound: routing ticks and subtle mechanism tone; final resolved impact/silence pocket on the delivered artifact.

## 4. Style bible for this sample

Palette:
- human surface: natural warm-neutral real-world color;
- transition/backend: darker neutral environment with controlled warm/cool functional separation, but no default cyan-magenta neon AI palette.

Material:
- real skin/device/glass at opening;
- paper/data/metal/frosted technical material mix in backend;
- physically legible occlusion, reflections and contact shadows where applicable.

Camera/lens grammar:
- opening: macro / shallow focus / human-scale proximity;
- transition: committed Z-axis crossing;
- backend: medium-to-wide reveal with a small parallax arc, then controlled push toward delivered result.

Motion grammar:
- fast manual micro-actions → one decisive acceleration → slower, confident causal routing → held payoff;
- no all-linear easing;
- no repeated scale-pop card entrances.

Typography:
- support only; never explain what the image fails to explain;
- maximum one short phrase in the final beat for this sample.

## 5. Engine routing

Preferred zero/low-incremental-cost route:
1. licensed/authorized stock or existing footage for the human/device opening;
2. NLE/compositor spatial treatment for plane-crossing and depth transition;
3. precise code/vector motion only for data strips, routing ticks and final type;
4. stock/created material assets + compositing for backend stations;
5. generative video only if a no-extra-cost entitlement is available and it materially improves the spatial shot.

No paid generation is authorized merely to complete this sample.

## 7. Hard VISUAL_PREVIEW acceptance

Score each 1–5 against the moving benchmark pack. Every hard dimension must be >=4 and Creative Director average >=4.25:

1. first-2-second stop power;
2. dominant focal event;
3. material/asset richness;
4. physical/spatial coherence;
5. meaningful camera/depth change;
6. transformation causality;
7. motion timing / holds / easing;
8. representative final fidelity;
9. advertiser showcase value;
10. picture/sound cohesion;
11. memorable hero moment;
12. absence of generic SaaS / AI-template feel.

Immediate reject if the sample can be summarized as “clean cards moving on a premium background.”

## 8. Production rule

Do not expand to the 59.5-second film from this contract alone. Render the actual sample, watch at normal speed, extract a contact sheet, compare side by side with the benchmark pack, and iterate until hard PASS. Only a passing rendered artifact may promote methods from candidate to validated technique.
