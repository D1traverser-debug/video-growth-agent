# Pilot 002 — Cinematic Benchmark Lane

Status: ACTIVE BENCHMARK CONTRACT  
Purpose: prevent motion-design fluency from being mistaken for cinematic image-making.

This lane complements the existing BUCK / Ordinary Folk / TAQA motion-and-explanation benchmarks. It does **not** ask Pilot 002 to imitate a film's surface style. It extracts film-production mechanisms that can be judged shot by shot.

## 1. Why this lane exists

Sample V2 proved causal staging and edit timing but remained visually procedural. The missing bar was not another UI treatment. It was image weight: human scale, physical light, material specificity, intentional lens/scale, spatial consequence and a memorable final image.

A quality-critical preview must therefore survive two separate comparisons:

1. **motion/explanation lane** — clarity, timing, causality, graphic precision;
2. **cinematic lane** — photographed-world plausibility, image weight, blocking, light/material integration, lens/scale intention and emotional progression.

Passing one lane cannot compensate for failing the other.

## 2. Film references and transferable mechanisms

### A. Blade Runner 2049 — Roger Deakins / Denis Villeneuve

Primary source: American Cinematographer, Roger Deakins discussion and production archive.

Source URLs:
- https://theasc.com/articles/roger-deakins-asc-bsc-blade-runner-2049-discussion
- https://theasc.com/articles/deakins-blade-runner-2049

Transferable mechanisms:

1. **Character/emotion precedes spectacle.** Deakins describes keeping the visual problem simple even at large scale: framing and lighting follow character and emotional purpose.
2. **Architecture is part of lighting.** Silhouette, moving light and spatial mood are designed through sets and architecture, not added as decorative glow.
3. **Practical/physical basis creates image weight.** Wherever possible, photographed elements and practical effects establish texture, detail and believable interaction before VFX extends them.
4. **Sequence palettes have a dramatic job.** Bold palette changes distinguish worlds/sequences rather than repeating one premium-tech grade everywhere.
5. **Storyboard without becoming mechanically trapped by it.** Preparation defines intent; performance and discovery may still change execution.

Pilot 002 consequence:
- the backend cannot be a dark UI corridor with prettier particles;
- its light must reveal spatial structure and causal movement;
- foreground human reality and backend world require materially different light/scale regimes;
- the hero frame must feel like a photographed place/event, not a designed dashboard.

### B. Arrival — Bradford Young / Denis Villeneuve

Primary source: American Cinematographer, `Universal Translator: Arrival`.

Source URL:
- https://theasc.com/article/arrival-cinematography-bradford-young/

Transferable mechanisms:

1. **Naturalism can carry science fiction.** The extraordinary world gains credibility from flesh-and-bone realism rather than constant futuristic decoration.
2. **Practical source logic.** Young describes a discipline where visible windows/lamps motivate the illumination instead of unmotivated beauty lighting.
3. **One frame first.** The production frequently preferred getting one deliberate frame right rather than collecting coverage for its own sake.
4. **Lens program is narrative, not cosmetic.** Focal lengths and proximity were chosen around how the character should feel in frame; memory material deliberately used a different lens/texture regime.
5. **Camera movement has a psychological assignment.** Much of the craft is procedural and restrained; a wandering camera is used specifically when disorientation is the point.
6. **Dailies can invalidate an approach.** When memory material was not working, the filmmakers explicitly admitted it and changed lens/proximity strategy rather than defending sunk work.

Pilot 002 consequence:
- first 2 seconds must begin in credible human-scale reality;
- camera motion cannot exist merely to advertise production value;
- the screen-plane break needs a clear psychological/narrative reason: manual cognitive load collapses into delegated agency;
- if normal-speed dailies do not communicate that change, the shot is redesigned rather than polished.

### C. Jeff Cronenweth — low-light discipline / material adaptation

Primary sources: American Cinematographer profiles and production discussion.

Source URLs:
- https://theasc.com/article/cronenweth-adventurous-eye/
- https://theasc.com/article/tales-from-the-loop-strange-machines/

Transferable mechanisms:

1. **Darkness is modeled, not empty.** Faces and objects remain shaped by controlled top/edge relationships rather than disappearing into generic black backgrounds.
2. **Technology choice follows the material.** Camera/lens systems are tested for the emotional and optical problem rather than selected because they are novel.
3. **No fixed house style.** Visual strategy adapts to story instead of repeating the creator's favorite look.

Pilot 002 consequence:
- avoid the recurring 'black premium AI space + gold accent' house style;
- backend darkness must contain readable depth, falloff, texture and motivated illumination;
- engine choice is downstream of shot requirement.

## 3. Shot contract for Sample V3

### Shot A — human/manual reality

Must establish within the first 0.5–2.0 seconds:
- recognizable hand/device/work surface or equally concrete human-scale action;
- one dominant focal event;
- tactile material variation: skin, glass, keycaps, paper/desk/device surface;
- motivated practical light;
- no floating HUD as the primary visual idea;
- macro/close framing that feels observed rather than diagrammed.

Failure if it could be described as `illustration of a laptop with labels`.

### Shot B — screen-plane rupture / delegation

Must show a physically coherent state change:
- a declared start state and end state;
- camera/motion direction with spatial consequence;
- foreground task fragments become delegated operations through visible cause/effect;
- transition must read muted;
- generative engine owns material/camera transformation; deterministic code may only own exact typography/data overlays/timing.

Failure if it is primarily `portal + particles + cards`.

### Shot C — backend/service world

Must feel like a different medium/world, not another UI scene:
- strong depth hierarchy;
- motivated moving or changing light;
- material surfaces with weight and occlusion;
- task movement changes the environment or travels through it causally;
- no repeated SaaS modules as the scene grammar.

Failure if the viewer can understand it only by reading labels.

### Shot D — prepared result / hero image

Must create the strongest still frame of the sample:
- materially different scale/composition from opening;
- one unmistakable result object/event;
- payoff visible before explanatory copy;
- poster/thumbnail-worthiness;
- no return to a generic result card.

Failure if removing the text destroys the payoff.

## 4. Cinematic review dimensions

Every VISUAL_PREVIEW reviewer receives these as supplemental evidence requirements. These do not replace the existing quality rubric.

### Director
- blocking / focal progression has a narrative reason;
- shot scale changes correspond to story escalation;
- camera movement has a psychological or explanatory assignment;
- the sequence has beginning → irreversible transition → payoff.

### Art Director
- architecture, material and illumination work as one system;
- practical/source logic is believable even when stylized;
- palette changes identify worlds without turning into arbitrary color grading;
- depth is produced by real occlusion/scale/light relationships, not glow layers.

### Motion Director
- motion transfers force/state rather than decorating surfaces;
- acceleration/deceleration communicates causal events;
- camera, object and light motion do not all compete at once;
- physical coherence survives normal-speed playback.

### Retention Editor
- first visual change occurs immediately;
- first two seconds create a specific unresolved promise;
- comprehension does not depend on captions;
- no visually flat interval is defended because the narration is informative.

### Creative Director
- compare actual normal-speed render side by side with both cinematic and motion/explanation lanes;
- reject 'technically elegant but emotionally weightless';
- reject any sample whose strongest description is its software technique rather than its image/event;
- no hard dimension below 4/5; quality-critical average >= 4.25/5.

## 5. Anti-copy / anti-style-transfer rule

Film references define **mechanisms and quality thresholds**, not a prompt to imitate protected characters, production design, frames or a living artist's signature style. Sample V3 must remain an original visual system for Pilot 002.

## 6. Promotion rule

This cinematic lane becomes production authority only when:

1. the actual rendered sample is reviewed at normal speed;
2. all configured VISUAL_PREVIEW work orders and immutable reviewer receipts exist;
3. each review is bound to the exact preview digest and reference-pack digests;
4. both the motion/explanation lane and this cinematic lane pass;
5. failed shots are repaired at shot/layer granularity before any 59.5-second rebuild.
